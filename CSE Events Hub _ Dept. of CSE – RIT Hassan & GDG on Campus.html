About the Hub

One home for every CSE department initiative

Designed for students, faculty coordinators, and GDG on Campus volunteers at Rajeev Institute of Technology, Hassan to discover events, register effortlessly, and follow live campus standings.

Department of Computer Science & Engineering, RIT Hassan

The Department of CSE fosters hands-on engineering practice alongside teamwork, athletic spirit, and cultural expression. From 24-hour coding sprints to inter-semester cricket and annual fests, every student has a stage to build and lead.

  • Unified schedule across Technical, Cultural, and Sports tracks
  • Instant solo and team registration with local persistence
  • Transparent live match scoreboard and batch points standings

GDG on Campus – RIT Hassan

Google Developer Groups on Campus empowers students with workshops, study jams, build challenges, and peer mentorship on web, cloud, and AI technologies.

Built for Open Participation

Sign in as a student to auto-fill registrations and view your entries, or log in as an event coordinator to edit live scores, update the points table, and post announcements.
